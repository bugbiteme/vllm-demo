# Prior Authorization Requirements

*Fictitious clinical/administrative policy for RAG testing purposes only. Applies to Meridian Health Partners commercial plans.*

Prior authorization (PA) is a pre-service review confirming that a planned service meets Meridian's medical necessity and benefit coverage criteria before it is rendered. Services performed without required PA are subject to denial (denial code CO-197) regardless of medical necessity, unless rendered in a documented emergency.

## Services Always Requiring Prior Authorization

| Service Category | Examples | Standard Review Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced imaging | MRI, MRA, CT, PET scans (non-emergency) | 2 business days |
| Inpatient admissions (non-emergency) | Elective surgery, scheduled inpatient procedures | 3 business days |
| Durable medical equipment over $1,000 | Power wheelchairs, CPAP with auto-titration | 5 business days |
| Specialty pharmacy drugs | Biologics, injectable oncology agents | 2 business days |
| Bariatric surgery | Gastric bypass, sleeve gastrectomy | 10 business days |
| Applied behavior analysis (ABA) therapy | Autism spectrum treatment plans | 5 business days |
| Transplant services | Solid organ and bone marrow transplant evaluation | 10 business days |
| Out-of-network referrals | Any elective service referred outside the Meridian network | 5 business days |

## Services Not Requiring Prior Authorization

- Emergency room visits and stabilization services.
- Preventive care and routine screenings.
- Primary care office visits.
- In-network maternity care through delivery (notification recommended but not required).
- Urgent care visits.

## Expedited (Urgent) Review

An expedited review (24-hour turnaround) is available when a standard timeframe could seriously jeopardize the member's life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, or would subject the member to severe, unmanaged pain. Expedited requests must be flagged as urgent by the requesting provider and include supporting clinical documentation.

## How to Submit a Prior Authorization Request

1. Submit via the Meridian Provider Portal (preferred) or fax to 555-0142.
2. Include: member ID, requested CPT/HCPCS code(s), supporting ICD-10 diagnosis, relevant clinical notes, and requesting provider NPI.
3. Incomplete requests are pended for up to 5 business days pending additional information before administrative denial.

## Peer-to-Peer Review

If a prior authorization request is denied, the requesting provider may request a peer-to-peer conversation with a Meridian medical director within 10 business days of the denial notice by calling the Utilization Management line at 555-0199. Peer-to-peer review does not replace the formal appeals process described in `member-appeals-grievance-process.md`.

## Retroactive (Post-Service) Authorization

Retroactive authorization requests are considered only for emergency admissions that convert to inpatient status, or when the member's eligibility was not verifiable at time of service. Retroactive requests must be submitted within 14 days of the date of service.
