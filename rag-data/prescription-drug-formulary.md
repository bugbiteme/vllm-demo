# Prescription Drug Formulary (Sample Excerpt)

*Fictitious formulary document for RAG testing purposes only. Applies to Meridian Complete PPO. Drug tier cost-sharing amounts reference `plan-benefits-summary.md`.*

## Tier Structure

| Tier | Description | Member Cost-Share |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Preferred generic drugs | $15 copay |
| Tier 2 | Preferred brand-name drugs | $45 copay |
| Tier 3 | Non-preferred brand-name drugs | $90 copay |
| Tier 4 | Specialty drugs (biologics, injectables, high-cost therapies) | 25% coinsurance, max $250/fill |

## Step Therapy Requirements

Certain Tier 3 and Tier 4 drugs require documented trial and failure of a lower-tier alternative before approval:

- **Ozempic (semaglutide):** Requires documented trial of metformin for at least 90 days, unless contraindicated.
- **Humira/Enbrel (biologics):** Requires documented trial of at least two conventional disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs), unless contraindicated.

## Specialty Pharmacy

All Tier 4 specialty drugs must be dispensed through Meridian's designated specialty pharmacy partner, MeridianRx Specialty. Specialty drugs filled at a standard retail pharmacy will not be covered, except for the initial emergency supply (up to 5 days) when clinically urgent.

## Formulary Exceptions

Providers may request a formulary exception for a non-covered drug or a tier exception for a lower cost-share when a covered alternative is contraindicated or has been ineffective. Exception requests follow the same submission process as prior authorization (see `prior-authorization-requirements.md`) and are reviewed within 2 business days for standard requests, 24 hours for expedited requests.
