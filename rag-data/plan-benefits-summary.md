## What Is Covered

- Preventive care (annual physicals, immunizations, screenings) at **no cost** when performed in-network.
- Inpatient hospital services, subject to deductible and 20% coinsurance after deductible.
- Outpatient surgery, subject to deductible and 20% coinsurance after deductible.
- Mental health and substance use disorder services at parity with medical/surgical benefits.
- Maternity care, including prenatal, delivery, and postnatal visits.
- Durable medical equipment (DME) at 20% coinsurance after deductible.
- Skilled nursing facility care, limited to 100 days per plan year.
- Home health care, limited to 60 visits per plan year.

## What Is Not Covered

- Cosmetic procedures not related to reconstructive necessity.
- Services from providers outside the Meridian network, except emergency care.
- Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by Meridian's Medical Policy Committee.
- Long-term custodial care.
- Elective procedures performed without required prior authorization (see `prior-authorization-requirements.md`).

## Member Cost-Sharing Notes

- Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum accumulate separately for in-network and out-of-network services; amounts do not cross-apply.
- Once the out-of-pocket maximum is met, Meridian pays 100% of covered in-network services for the remainder of the plan year.
- Copays for primary care and specialist visits do **not** count toward the deductible but do count toward the out-of-pocket maximum.
