# Care Management Programs

*Fictitious program guide for RAG testing purposes only. Describes Meridian Health Partners member care coordination and chronic disease management offerings.*

## Overview

Meridian's Care Management team provides no-cost, opt-in support programs for members with chronic or complex health conditions. Enrollment is voluntary and can be initiated by the member, a treating provider, or automatically suggested based on claims/utilization patterns (with member consent required to proceed).

## Diabetes Management Program

- **Eligibility:** Members with an ICD-10 diagnosis of Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes in the last 12 months.
- **Services included:** Dedicated care coordinator, quarterly A1C monitoring reminders, no-cost diabetic supplies coordination, nutritional counseling referral, and access to the Meridian Diabetes Digital Coaching app.
- **Goal metrics tracked:** A1C trend, annual eye exam completion, annual foot exam completion, annual nephropathy screening.

## Cardiac Care Program

- **Eligibility:** Members with a recent cardiac event (MI, CABG, PCI) or a diagnosis of heart failure, CAD, or uncontrolled hypertension.
- **Services included:** Post-discharge follow-up call within 48 hours, medication reconciliation support, cardiac rehabilitation referral coordination, remote blood pressure monitoring device (member keeps at no cost).

## Maternity Care Program (Meridian Healthy Beginnings)

- **Eligibility:** Automatically offered upon claims indicating a confirmed pregnancy; members may also self-enroll.
- **Services included:** Dedicated maternity care coordinator, high-risk pregnancy screening and coordination, postpartum depression screening at 6 weeks, breastfeeding support referral, and a $150 postpartum wellness incentive for completing the program's care plan milestones.

## Behavioral Health Integration Program

- **Eligibility:** Members with a behavioral health diagnosis who also have two or more chronic medical conditions.
- **Services included:** Coordination between behavioral health and primary care providers, medication reconciliation across prescribers, and crisis line access (555-0911, available 24/7).

## Complex Case Management

For members with catastrophic, high-cost, or multi-system conditions (e.g., transplant candidates, members with 3+ hospital admissions in 6 months), Meridian assigns a Registered Nurse Case Manager who coordinates across all treating providers, reviews the member's full care plan monthly, and serves as a single point of contact for the member and family.

## How to Enroll

- Member Portal → "Care Programs" → Select program → Submit enrollment.
- Call Care Management directly at 555-0155.
- Provider referral via the Provider Portal "Care Management Referral" form.

## Program Withdrawal

Members may withdraw from any Care Management program at any time without affecting their underlying plan benefits, by notifying their care coordinator or through the Member Portal.
