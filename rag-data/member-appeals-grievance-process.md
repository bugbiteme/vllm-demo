# Member and Provider Appeals & Grievance Process

*Fictitious process document for RAG testing purposes only. Applies to Meridian Health Partners commercial plans.*

## Definitions

- **Appeal:** A formal request to reconsider an adverse benefit determination, such as a denied claim or denied prior authorization.
- **Grievance:** A formal complaint about any matter other than an adverse benefit determination, such as service quality, wait times, or provider conduct.

## Who May File

- The member.
- The member's authorized representative (with signed authorization on file).
- The treating provider, on the member's behalf, for clinical appeals (e.g., medical necessity denials).

## Filing an Appeal

### Level 1 — Internal Appeal

- **Deadline to file:** 180 days from the date of the adverse determination notice.
- **How to file:** Written request via the Member Portal, mail to Meridian Appeals Department, PO Box 44299, Meridian, ST 55501, or fax to 555-0177.
- **Required content:** Member ID, claim number or authorization reference number, reason for appeal, and any supporting documentation (medical records, provider letters).
- **Standard timeframe for decision:** 30 calendar days for pre-service (prior authorization) appeals; 60 calendar days for post-service (claims) appeals.
- **Expedited appeals:** Available when a standard timeframe could seriously jeopardize the member's life or health. Decision issued within 72 hours.

### Level 2 — Second-Level Internal Appeal

If the Level 1 appeal is denied, the member or provider may request a second-level review by a different medical director than the one who issued the Level 1 decision.

- **Deadline to file:** 60 days from the Level 1 denial notice.
- **Standard timeframe for decision:** 30 calendar days.

### External Review

If the Level 2 internal appeal is denied and the denial involves a medical necessity, experimental/investigational, or similar clinical determination, the member may request an independent external review by an accredited Independent Review Organization (IRO).

- **Deadline to file:** 120 days from the Level 2 denial notice.
- **Timeframe for decision:** 45 calendar days for standard review; 72 hours for expedited review.
- **Outcome:** The IRO's decision is binding on Meridian.

## Filing a Grievance

Grievances may be filed at any time via the Member Portal, by calling Member Services at 555-0100, or in writing to the Appeals Department address above. Meridian will acknowledge receipt within 5 business days and issue a resolution within 30 calendar days.

## What to Include With a Clinical Appeal

- A letter of medical necessity from the treating provider.
- Relevant clinical notes, imaging reports, or lab results supporting the requested service.
- Any peer-reviewed literature supporting the treatment, if the denial was based on experimental/investigational status.
- A completed Appeal Request Form (available on the Member Portal).

## Status Tracking

Members and providers can check appeal status in real time through the Member Portal or Provider Portal under "Appeals & Grievances." Status updates include: Received, Under Clinical Review, Additional Information Requested, and Decision Issued.
